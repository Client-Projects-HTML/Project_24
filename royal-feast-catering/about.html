Our Story

Fifteen Years Of Cooking For The City's Biggest Days

Royal Feast started in 2011 when founder Chef Anand Verma began cooking wedding lunches for family friends out of a single home kitchen. Word spread, and within three years we'd outgrown that kitchen twice over.

Today we run three commercial kitchens, employ over 60 full-time staff and have catered more than 1,000 events — from 20-guest anniversary dinners to 800-guest weddings. What hasn't changed is the recipe: fresh ingredients, honest pricing and a chef on-site at every event.
