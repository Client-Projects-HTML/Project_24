About Royal Feast

A Family Kitchen That Grew Into The City's Most Trusted Caterer

What began in 2011 as a single home kitchen preparing wedding lunches for neighbours is today a full-service catering house with three commercial kitchens, an in-house decor team and a roster of chefs trained across Indian, Chinese and continental cuisine.

  • Hygienic, FSSAI-certified kitchens
  • Custom menu planning for any cuisine or diet
  • On-time setup, service and cleanup, guaranteed
