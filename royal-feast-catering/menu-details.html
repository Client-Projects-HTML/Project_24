Hyderabadi Dum Biryani

Long-grain basmati rice layered with marinated chicken or mutton, slow-cooked in a sealed handi over a charcoal dum for that signature smoky aroma. Served with mirchi ka salan and a cooling raita.

₹460 per guest
Spice LevelMedium (adjustable)
Serving Size250g per guest
Add To My Event Menu

Ingredients

  • Aged basmati rice, hand-sorted for long grains
  • Chicken or mutton, marinated 8 hours in yoghurt & spices
  • Saffron, fried onions, mint and coriander
  • Whole spices: cardamom, cinnamon, star anise, mace

Allergen Information

Contains dairy (ghee, yoghurt). Prepared in a kitchen that also handles nuts and gluten. A fully nut-free preparation is available on request — please flag any allergies on your enquiry form.
