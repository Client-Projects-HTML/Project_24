Overview

A Full-Service Wedding Kitchen, On Site

Our wedding catering service covers everything from the mehendi lunch to the reception dinner: menu design across multiple cuisines, live cooking counters, dedicated waitstaff and complete setup and cleanup. A senior event chef is assigned to your wedding from the first tasting through the final course.

We cater intimate 100-guest weddings and full-scale 1000-guest celebrations with the same attention to timing, hygiene and presentation.

Up to 1000 guests 3-6 hour service window
